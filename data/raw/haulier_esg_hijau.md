Hijau Transport Sdn Bhd - Sustainability and Conduct Policy 2026 (FICTIONAL SAMPLE)

1. Climate. Hijau Transport commits to reduce CO2 emissions per tonne-kilometre by 40 percent by 2030 against a 2023 baseline, validated against a science-based pathway.

2. Fleet. Our fleet consists of Euro 6 diesel prime movers and LNG-powered prime movers, with an average vehicle age below four years. Two electric prime movers are under trial for short port runs.

3. Measurement. We measure fuel consumption per trip with telematics and report fuel use and CO2 emissions to every customer quarterly.

4. Certification. Our Port Klang depot is certified to ISO 14001 environmental management and ISO 45001 occupational health and safety.

5. People. Drivers work a maximum of 10 hours per day with mandatory rest periods. Forced labour, recruitment fees and passport retention are prohibited under our code of conduct.

6. Safety. All drivers complete dangerous goods training and refresher certification every two years before carrying chemical cargo.
