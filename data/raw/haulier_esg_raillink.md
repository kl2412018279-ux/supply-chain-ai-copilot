RailLink Intermodal - Environmental and Social Policy (FICTIONAL SAMPLE)

1. Modal shift. Moving containers by rail emits far less CO2 per tonne-kilometre than road haulage. RailLink measures and reports CO2 savings to customers for every booking.

2. Target. We aim to cut emissions from our first and last mile trucking by 25 percent by 2028 through Euro 5 and Euro 6 partner fleets.

3. People. Partner hauliers must follow our supplier code of conduct, including legal working hours and no forced labour.

4. Safety. Terminal staff handling dangerous goods receive dangerous goods training annually.
