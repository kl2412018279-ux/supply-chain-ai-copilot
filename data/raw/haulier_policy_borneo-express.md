Borneo Express Shipping - Service and Responsibility Statement (FICTIONAL SAMPLE)

1. Service. Borneo Express operates a fast weekly feeder service from Port Klang to Kuching and Kota Kinabalu.

2. Quality. We are preparing for a quality management certification audit and plan to complete it in 2027.

3. Safety. Officers hold valid STCW certification and dangerous goods training is provided to cargo officers.

4. Liability. Cargo liability is limited to the bill of lading terms and covered by our P&I club.
