Cempaka Haulage - Company Policy Statement (FICTIONAL SAMPLE)

1. Service. Cempaka Haulage provides container haulage from Westport and Northport to industrial areas in the Klang Valley, Negeri Sembilan and Perak.

2. Safety. Vehicles are inspected monthly and drivers must wear safety vests and helmets inside port areas.

3. Fleet. Our fleet of prime movers is maintained by our in-house workshop to keep costs competitive. Prime movers are fitted with GPS units so the office can locate a truck on request.

4. Claims. Damage claims are handled case by case; customers are advised to arrange their own cargo cover.

5. Customers. We aim to respond to all booking requests within two hours during office hours.
