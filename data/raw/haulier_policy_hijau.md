Hijau Transport Sdn Bhd - Operations and Compliance Policy 2026 (FICTIONAL SAMPLE)

1. Fleet. Our fleet consists of Euro 6 diesel prime movers with an average vehicle age below four years. Every prime mover and trailer follows a preventive maintenance schedule every 10,000 km at our Port Klang workshop.

2. Drivers. Drivers work a maximum of 10 hours per day with mandatory rest breaks every four hours, and our fatigue management programme removes drivers from duty after any night shift.

3. Dangerous goods. All drivers complete dangerous goods training and refresher certification every two years before carrying chemical cargo.

4. Tracking. Every truck carries a GPS unit with real-time tracking shared with customers through our booking portal.

5. Insurance. We hold goods-in-transit insurance of RM 500,000 per container and cargo liability cover for all subcontracted moves.

6. Certification. Our Port Klang depot is certified to ISO 9001 quality management, ISO 45001 occupational health and safety and ISO 39001 road traffic safety.
