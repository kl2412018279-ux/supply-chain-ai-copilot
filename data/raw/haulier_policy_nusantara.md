Nusantara Coastal Line - Fleet and Compliance Statement (FICTIONAL SAMPLE)

1. Compliance. Our feeder vessels comply with IMO SOLAS and the ISPS Code for port security.

2. Tracking. Every vessel transmits its position by AIS, and customers can follow each voyage on our website.

3. Crew. Crew welfare follows the Maritime Labour Convention, including rest hours and repatriation rights.

4. Safety. Dangerous goods are stowed according to the IMDG Code and crew complete dangerous goods training.

5. Liability. Cargo liability is covered by our P&I club under the Hague-Visby Rules.
