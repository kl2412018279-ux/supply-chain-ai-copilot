RailLink Intermodal - Service and Compliance Policy (FICTIONAL SAMPLE)

1. Service. RailLink moves containers by KTMB block train between Port Klang, Ipoh and Prai, with partner hauliers for the first and last mile.

2. Partner fleet. First and last mile moves use partner hauliers with Euro 5 and Euro 6 prime movers.

3. Tracking. Every wagon and partner truck reports its position by GPS, and customers see real-time tracking from terminal gate-in to delivery.

4. Safety. Terminal staff handling dangerous goods receive dangerous goods training annually.

5. Certification. Our Ipoh and Prai terminals are certified to ISO 9001 quality management.
